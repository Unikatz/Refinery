# Validated Human-Centric Heuristics
> Distilled principles from past cycles that keep our products grounded in human dignity, low cognitive load, and resource respect.

- **Heuristic 1 (Cognitive Respect):** Never force a human to configure what the system can safely infer or default.
- **Heuristic 2 (Graceful Recovery):** Error states must explain *why* in plain language and offer a 1-click path forward.
- **Heuristic 3 (Resource Stewardship):** Treat the user's attention, battery, bandwidth, and privacy as finite, sacred resources.
